Chronological selection check

At each boundary choose training net profit minus half realized drawdown, requiring at least 40 resolved training setups, at least 12 in each training month and at least 50% wins. Fit Aug–Sep for October, then Aug–Oct for November. The selected rules earned $639.17 across October and the historical November portion, compared with $1729.30 for baseline. Boundary outcomes are truncated to information available at selection. This selection procedure did not improve later profit. It is itself retrospective because the history has been reused.

The 30% body filter was not the chronological winner. The former Nov18–20 sample excludes no setups under this filter, so it provides no new evidence for the filter. Only four avoided losses and one missed winner explain the gain.

A descriptive paired-day bootstrap for the filter’s incremental profit gives a 95% range of $-89.55 to $386.82; it does not account for the many settings tried or prior reuse of these data.

Methods and verification

Reused the verified exhaustive OHLC event-order replay. Historical execution uses M15; the former fresh period uses M1. Confirmation features use only candles closed by confirmation; reference geometry inherits the archived next-open model. ATR14 is a simple mean of the latest 14 true ranges, not Wilder smoothing; missing required contiguous bars reject the corresponding filter. Relative volume uses 19 prior bars; M15 SMA20 includes confirmation. Body fraction requires only the final confirmation candle. Per-leg targets are measured from original reference; pending entries retire at their own target, SL or deadline.

Baseline profit and 119-win/87-loss counts reproduced exactly. Independent Python checks reconcile selected trade cash flows, monthly totals and sensitivity costs. The risk-budget assertions and closed-candle feature checks passed. Full JSON includes every tested combination and selected per-entry records.
