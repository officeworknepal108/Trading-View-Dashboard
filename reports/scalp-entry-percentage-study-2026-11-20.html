Scalp 1:1 entry-percentage study

235 archived XAUUSD setups, August 3?November 18, 2026. BUY entries below the original reference; SELL entries above it. Original SL and TP stay fixed.

Finding: 0% first entry with a second entry around 60?75% is an exploratory candidate. The training-selected 0%/75% pair achieved 61.93% full-period wins versus 59.78% for 10%/25%, but earned less in later-period validation. There is no demonstrated reliable optimum.

Dollar model: fixed $4 total risk allowance per setup (illustrative $200 balance at 2%), 60% allocated to first entry and 40% to second. No compounding. Fractional lots, zero fees/spread/slippage, no account-level restrictions. These are simulated gross results, not attainable Account 2 returns. With the observed Exness 0.01-lot minimum, all 234 setups with next-open data in the selected candidates fail the $4 sizing budget, including second-only fallback. The other setup lacks next-open data.

Method and limitations

  • candidates: 165 pairs. First entry 0â€“50%, second entry greater than first through 100%, both in 5-percentage-point increments. Percent of confirmation body measured from original reference. 0% means reference entry.
  • unchanged: Original fixed SL/TP, BUY lower and SELL higher, 60/40 total-risk allocation, 75-minute entry window, archived qualifying signals, 300-pip per-leg maximum. Invalid entries beyond SL are skipped; unused risk is not transferred in ideal sizing.
  • selection: Before grid results: at least 70 resolved training setups (70% of baseline), at least 112 training setups with both geometrically valid entries (90% of available setups). Profit winner maximizes certain-result net R. Win winner maximizes setup win rate. Balanced score averages training percentile rank for conservative lower-bound net R and win rate.
  • counting: One signal is one setup. TP/SL needs at least one definitely filled leg and no uncertain leg outcome. Percentages exclude uncertain/open/unfilled/no-entry setups. Whole uncertain/open setups excluded from main dollar totals. Also report loose pessimistic/optimistic R bounds for unresolved OHLC paths.
  • dollars: Illustrative $200 capital, fixed $4 setup risk, no compounding. Ideal fractional lots; no spreads, fees, slippage, margin, daily-loss limits, concurrent-trade caps or half-risk overlap adjustments. Cash is gross research P&L, not executable account returns.
  • minimumLotSensitivity: Separate sizing-only sensitivity uses current Exness XAUUSDm contract 100 oz, .01 minimum/step, fixed $4 budget. If either split lot is too small, skip first and try second with full budget, rounded down. Not a broker replay: bid/ask, tick rounding, commissions, margin and account-level controls are not modeled.
  • limits: Exploratory historical tuning on one instrument and 235 archived qualified setups. Later-period validation is a chronological check, not pristine independent unseen data: the baseline was already reviewed. Many parameter comparisons increase selection bias. No future performance guarantee or live strategy change.

Validation: the 10%/25% replay reproduces the earlier corrected-reference study, original exits are checked against archived levels, all valid corrected entries must have RR at least 1:1, and outcome counts reconcile.
