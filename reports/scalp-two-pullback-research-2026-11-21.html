Two genuine pullback entries — $50 total setup risk

Both entries wait for a price pullback after the original setup confirms. Keep the original SL; each entry is closer to that SL. No delayed market entry. Maintain the 30% minimum confirmation-body filter. Two pending limit orders are planned; one or both may remain unfilled.

Offline research only. No protected dashboard source, live settings or orders changed. This round tested 120 policies on previously reused Aug3–Nov20 prices. Selected results are hindsight candidates, not unseen validation. Fixed risk, no compounding. Gross figures exclude costs and uncertain outcomes.

Interpretation and checks

Both versions meet the two-pullback requirement. Exact-per-entry1.25R gives a separate target per ticket and lower modeled profit than the earlier common-target baseline. A common target produces higher per-entry RR after a pullback; calling that exactly1.25R from each entry would be incorrect. Deeper entries reduce fills and can increase drawdown even with a shorter price distance to SL.

Retrospective selection from earlier months used cost-aware net minus half drawdown, min30 resolved training setups and min8 per training month. Its October plus historical-November result was $1187.49, versus $1132.36 for the strict-size benchmark. This small later-period difference is reused-data exploration, not independent validation.
