Scalp improvement research — $50 risk, $5,000 starting balance

Finding: 70 focused variants did not establish a reliably better replacement. Keeping 0%/10% entries, 30%/70% allocation and a 30-minute deadline but moving the common reference target from 1.25R to 1.5R slightly increased idealized total profit, while reducing win rate and increasing realized drawdown.

Matched-outcome uncertainty check

On setups resolved by both rules, the ideal difference is $242.82. A descriptive 5,000-resample paired-day interval is $-534.60 to $955.87. Exploratory descriptive interval on matched resolved setups only. Does not correct for 70-rule selection, serial dependence across days, excluded uncertainty, or prior reuse of data. Not independent significance evidence.

Conclusion

Retain the 1.25R baseline for further research. Treat 1.5R as an exploratory alternative; its small extra modeled profit does not compensate clearly for lower accuracy and higher drawdown. A frozen rule tested on new, unseen prices with actual broker costs is needed before claiming improvement.
