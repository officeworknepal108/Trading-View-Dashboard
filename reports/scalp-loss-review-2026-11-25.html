Market context

The BEA PCE and GDP release was scheduled for 19:15 Nepal. The reviewed losses all closed before that time; the latest was 18:12. It cannot explain these losses as a direct post-release reaction. OANDA documents that widening spreads can trigger stops, but this review lacks entry-time bid/ask ticks to establish whether that happened. CME lists Thanksgiving holiday schedules for November 26?28; holiday liquidity is a hypothesis requiring observed spreads, not a proven cause.

Decision

Keep the current live exits for now. The most focused next experiment is conditional breakeven with unchanged position risk, original protective stops and fixed 1.25R targets. Freeze the rule before collecting new demo setups; compare net expectancy, full-stop count, stopped-out eventual winners, drawdown and broker costs. Do not widen stops or increase risk to recover yesterday?s losses.

Limits of this research

  • Exploratory analysis of saved setups; missing, rejected and unfilled signals are not exhaustively reconstructed. Historical records may reflect changing strategy versions. These totals do not establish full-strategy performance.
  • Nov25 was inspected before hypotheses were tested. Earlier periods are comparison samples, not an untouched validation set. Conditional breakeven is a post-hoc hypothesis.
  • M1 replay uses current primary-broker bid candles with saved chart/MT5 entry levels. Chart snapshots are midpoint-like and tick-volume histories differ by feed. Clock alignment checked against three saved M15 snapshots (median close differences 0.08, 0.095 and 0.135 price units).
  • Both high/low intrabar paths were evaluated for stop/target/breakeven sequencing. Simulation begins at the first whole minute after entry; first-minute movements, bid/ask spread, commission, latency and subsequent sizing changes are not fully modelled. Gross R is before costs; breakeven can still lose cash.
  • Broader pre-entry-filter results use stored outcomes, not a rerun with alternative entries. Nine covered setups have only one stored leg. Chart and broker observations are not interchangeable with realized account P/L.
  • No source code, live orders, trading configuration or stop-loss settings changed.
