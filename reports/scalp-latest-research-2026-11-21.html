Latest scalp research: stops and delayed entries

Research finding: Waiting one M15 candle and using a single market entry at the next open produced a higher gross historical profit and win rate, with positive gross results in all four studied months. It also increased drawdown. This is an exploratory candidate, not an established live improvement.

672 stop-policy combinations and 240 timing-policy combinations were examined in this round, after earlier searches on the same prices. Winners were selected with hindsight. All prices have been reused; no independent unseen evaluation. Fixed $50 risk per setup on an illustrative $5,000 balance, no compounding. Market execution, costs, margin and position limits need separate verification. Live trading settings and strategy source were not changed.

Exact delayed-entry rule

  1. Retain the original M15 setup qualification. Require the final confirmation body to be at least 30% of its high-to-low range.
  2. Wait for the next complete M15 candle. At the opening of the following candle (15 minutes after original confirmation), inspect whether the original SL or the original-reference 1.25R target was touched during the wait. Cancel if either was touched or the history has a gap.
  3. Enter once at that new candle open, any candle colour. Keep the original pattern SL and original-reference 1.25R target unchanged. The target is not recalculated to 1.25R from the later entry; actual reward/risk varies.
  4. Risk at most $50 total using lot size calculated from the new entry-to-original-stop distance. Skip invalid geometry, risk over the inherited 300-pip entry cap, or below-minimum lot sizing. No second pullback, BE, trailing or early close.

The search used two orders at identical prices and exits. Their ideal outcomes are mathematically equivalent to one order with total $50 risk. This report independently calculates one-order lot rounding and costs; those values differ from flooring two orders separately. No claim is made that a future live market order fills at candle-open mid price.

Stop study result

The highest stop-study profit used 75% of original reference-to-SL distance, target 2R from revised reference risk, 0%/10% entries, 30%/70% allocation and body ≥30%. Gross profit $3828.47, win rate 45.54%, resolved drawdown $852.06. The higher profit came with lower accuracy and substantially higher drawdown, so it is not the primary candidate.

Verification and limits

The existing exhaustive OHLC event-order engine preserves unknown paths and gaps. Historical replay uses M15; the former Nov18–20 sample uses M1. Both studies reproduce the original 119 wins, 87 losses and $3,186.20 gross result. Assertions check closed-candle timing, unchanged original targets in delayed fixed-target mode, <=$50 allocated risk, monthly and exit-cash reconciliation, and equivalence of identical-price orders with one ideal order. Independent single-order calculations passed.

Qualification remains based on the previously archived setup universe. The new rules deliberately change execution timing or stops. No live executable bid/ask or slippage replay, broker tick rounding, margin model, aggregate-open-risk limit or daily-loss limit is included. All-positive gross months do not guarantee future positive months.
